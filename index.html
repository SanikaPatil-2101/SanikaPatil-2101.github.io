Development Pipeline

End-to-End System Architecture

How mechanical concepts translate into fully operational, simulated, and cloud-monitored automation solutions.

01

Parametric CAD Design

Drafting link geometries in Fusion 360 / SolidWorks, accounting for bearings, servo horns, fastener clearances, and center-of-mass distributions.

02

URDF Kinematic Modeling

Extracting mesh collision hulls and inertial matrices to form unified Xacro trees with mimic joints for RViz2, Gazebo, and web Three.js digital twins.

03

Embedded Electronics & Bridge

Writing C++ firmware for ESP32 and Arduino, implementing 50 Hz PWM angle mapping, serial synchronization protocols, and optical relay isolation.

04

Cloud Telemetry & Automation

Bridging hardware metrics to Blynk Cloud and industrial web telemetry dashboards with non-volatile flash checkpoints, energy formulas, and charts.
